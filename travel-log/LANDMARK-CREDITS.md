Aliases (no new drawing): Vatican City Q237 and the Sistine Chapel Q2943 use St. Peter's Q12512; the Eilean Donan islet Q79182 uses the castle Q20816698; the Acropolis of Athens Q131013 uses the traced Parthenon Q10288 (credited above). Rome, Athens, Cairo, Istanbul, Barcelona and Edinburgh city stamps reuse their landmark's drawing. v117: Angkor Q163607 uses Angkor Wat; Al-Khazneh Q1259626 uses Petra; Q65961372 (The Great Wall) uses the Great Wall. Cusco, Beijing and San Francisco city stamps reuse theirs. v118: Red Square Q41116 uses the traced St. Basil's Q129846 (credited above). New York (Statue of Liberty), Seattle (Space Needle), Toronto (CN Tower), Rio (Christ the Redeemer) and New Delhi (India Gate) city stamps reuse their traced icons; Paris already shows the Eiffel Tower. The Kaaba is deliberately not drawn. v119: the Khafre Q208358 and Menkaure Q238623 pyramids use our Giza drawing; Cairo's city stamp picks it up too. The traced Giza icon (Q37200, Q12508, listed above) is no longer shown: we replaced it with our own drawing, and its row stays here as a record.
