Privacy policy

Draft, last updated October 24, 2026. Plain-English summary; not yet reviewed by a lawyer.

Short version: Famstamp is a family travel log run by parents. Kids don't sign up or give us anything. Your trips stay on your phone unless a parent turns on Family Sync. We don't sell data, show ads or use third-party trackers, and you can delete everything.

Who we are

Famstamp is made by Dark Lighthouse Solutions ("we"). Questions about this policy or your family's data: privacy@darklighthousesolutions.com.

Kids and parents

  • The parent owns the account. The family account belongs to the parent or guardian who sets it up. Kids never sign up and are never asked to type personal information (no email, phone number, address, school or full name). Everything about a kid is entered, and can be changed or deleted, by the parent.
  • The app is meant to be set up and run by a parent or other adult. Kids' profiles (a name or nickname, an optional birth year and an avatar) are created and managed by that adult.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Kids can't create an account. If a kid joins a synced family, they do it with a code their parent makes, as a "kid" member the parent controls; we don't ask kids for an email address.
  • Parents can see, change, export and delete everything about their kids at any time (see Deleting). To ask us anything about your child's data, email privacy@darklighthousesolutions.com.
  • If you think a child gave us personal information without a parent, tell us and we'll delete it.

What stays on your device

By default, everything you add (people, trips, places, stamps, journal pages, drawings and photos) is stored only in your browser's storage on your phone or computer. We can't see it. Removing the app or clearing site data erases it, so keep a backup (Back up, or Help & feedback > Export everything).

What's stored in the cloud (only with Family Sync)

If a parent turns on Family Sync, a copy of the family's log is kept in a private database so family members' phones can share it. It is hosted by our database provider, Supabase, in the US. Photos are uploaded as smaller copies; full-size originals stay on your phone. It holds:

  • the sign-in email of adults who add one, or a Google sign-in if chosen (people who join from an invite code can use an anonymous sign-in with no email);
  • the family name, members and their roles (organizer, adult, kid);
  • people, trips, places, stamps, journal pages and settings you add;
  • copies of your photos, in private storage only family members can open;
  • if you turn on reminders, a push "subscription" address from your browser so we can send the notification.

Only members of your family can read your family's data; database rules enforce this. Optional features like a grandparent "follow" link or a Cousin Cup share only what they say on screen, and you can turn them off.

Photos and location

  • When you add a photo, the app shrinks it and saves a new copy. That copy doesn't keep the camera's hidden details (EXIF), such as GPS location or camera model.
  • Places come from what you type or pick on a map. If you tap "near me", your device's location is used on your device to find nearby places and isn't stored by us.
  • To show weather for a trip, the app asks Open-Meteo (a free weather service) about the place and date. It doesn't send your name or anything else about you.

Anonymous usage counts and error reports

To fix bugs and see which features get used, the app can send:

  • Usage counts: how many times each screen was opened per day (for example "passport: 12"). No names, ids or locations.
  • Error reports: the app version, the error message (with emails, long numbers and ids removed), which kind of screen, and the device type such as "Android Chrome".

Neither includes a user id, device id or anything you entered. We don't store IP addresses with them (a one-day scrambled code is used only to limit abuse of the waitlist). You can turn both off in Help & feedback.

Who we share with

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't use it for ads. We use a few service providers only to run the app: Supabase (database, sign-in, photo storage), our web host, your browser's push service (if you turn on reminders), Google (only if you choose Google sign-in), and Open-Meteo (weather, no personal data). If you buy a paid plan in future, the checkout provider (Polar, as merchant of record) handles payment and receives your billing details.

Emails

We email you only for sign-in codes and, if you joined the waitlist, launch news. Every waitlist email will let you unsubscribe.

Seeing, exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: Help & feedback > Export everything downloads a zip of all your data and photos.
  • Delete in the app: the family organizer can choose Family > Leave or delete > Delete my account and data. This deletes the family's cloud copy, photos and the organizer's sign-in. Other adults can delete their own sign-in or leave.
  • Delete by email: see how to request deletion.

Deleted cloud data is removed right away from the live database. Our database provider may keep encrypted backups for a short time before they expire.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, cloud data is protected by per-family access rules, and photos are in private storage. No system is perfect; if something goes wrong we'll tell affected families.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app first.
