Open Famstamp in Chrome

Your family's maps and trips stay on your own phone. No account needed.

  1. Open Famstamp in Chrome with the button above.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Install. It opens like any other app.
Already installed the old download?

Keep using it. Your maps and trips stay put. To be safe, tap Back up in the app now and then and save the file.

On an iPhone or iPad?

Open the app in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
