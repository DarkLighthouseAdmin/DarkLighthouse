Delete your account and data

Draft, last updated October 24, 2026.

You can delete your Famstamp data yourself in the app, or ask us to do it.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Optional: save a copy first in Help & feedback > Export everything.
  2. Family organizer: open Family > Leave or delete > Delete my account and data, type DELETE and confirm. This deletes the family's cloud copy (people, trips, stamps, journals, photo copies), any follow or Cup links, and your sign-in.
  3. Other adults: on the same screen you can leave the family or delete your own sign-in. The family's shared trips stay with the organizer.
  4. Not using Family Sync? Your data is only on your device. Use Back up > Start over or remove the app and clear the site's data.

By email

Email privacy@darklighthousesolutions.com (or support@darklighthousesolutions.com) from the email address you sign in with, and say "please delete my account and data". Parents can ask the same for a child's profile. We'll confirm it's you, delete the data within 30 days (usually much sooner) and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted and what's kept

  • Deleted: your sign-in, your family's synced people, trips, places, stamps, journal pages, drawings, photo copies, reminders, follow links and Cousin Cup entries.
  • Kept: anonymous usage counts and error reports, which never contained anything that identifies you. Short-lived database backups expire on their own.
  • If you joined the waitlist, ask us to remove that email too.
