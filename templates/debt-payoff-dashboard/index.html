Questions

Snowball or avalanche: which should I pick?

Avalanche (highest APR first) usually costs less interest. Snowball (smallest balance first) clears whole cards sooner, which some people find easier to stick with. The Compare tab shows both side by side with your own numbers.

Does it work for loans, not just cards?

Yes. Any debt with a balance, an interest rate and a minimum payment works, up to 10 in total.

Do I need Excel or any add-ons?

No. A free Google account is all you need. No scripts or add-ons.
