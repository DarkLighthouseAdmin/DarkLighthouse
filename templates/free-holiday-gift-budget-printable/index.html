Free Holiday Gift Budget Printable

One page to set your number for the season and keep track as you go: who you're buying for, what you planned, what you spent, and the extras that sneak up on you.

FreeDownload the PDF
  • Direct download. No email, no sign-up, no checkout
  • One US Letter page, prints fine on A4
  • Gift list, extras (food, travel, decor, cards) and a season total
  • Two example rows from a made-up family, the Riveras

How to use it

  1. Write your total budget at the top, plus what you've already saved.
  2. Divide what's left by the paychecks before the big day. That's what to set aside each payday.
  3. List everyone you're buying for with a budget next to each name, before you shop.
  4. Fill in what you spent as you go, and tick Got and Wrapped.
  5. Don't skip "Beyond gifts". Food, travel and decor are where most holiday budgets go over.
