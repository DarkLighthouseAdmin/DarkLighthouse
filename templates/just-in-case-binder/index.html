How it works

  1. Buy, and download the PDFs and spreadsheet link from your email.
  2. Start with the "gather these first" list in any section. You don't have to do it all in one sitting.
  3. Type into the PDF, or print it and write by hand.
  4. Tell one trusted person where it lives. Review it once a year.

Questions

Is this a will or a legal document?

No. It's an organizer, not a will, trust or legal document, and it isn't legal advice. Talk to an attorney in your state about wills, powers of attorney and advance directives.

Should I write my passwords in it?

No. It asks where your password manager is and the last 4 digits of accounts, never full account numbers or passwords.

Do I need special software?

No. Type in it with free Adobe Acrobat Reader or Mac Preview, or print it. The companion works in free Google Sheets or Excel.

Can I print it on A4?

Yes. The printable version comes in both US Letter and A4.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. At checkout, enter the email of the person you're giving it to, and the download goes straight to them.
