How it works

  1. Buy, then open the PDF in your email and click "Make a copy". The sheet is saved privately in your own Google Drive.
  2. Look around the example rows (a made-up family, the Riveras), then delete them.

  4. Add gifts and extras as you go. The Summary tells you where you're over or under.
