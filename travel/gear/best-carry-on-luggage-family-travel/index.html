Carry-on luggage for family travel: what fits, and what's worth buying

Flying carry-on with kids saves time at both ends and means nothing gets lost. The catch is that cabin bag limits change from airline to airline, and Europe's budget airlines are much stricter than US ones. Start with the limits, then pick bags that fit them. Carry-on suitcases and packs for the grown-ups

Ridge Carry-On

German Makrolon polycarbonate shell; exterior 22.5 × 14.5 × 9 in, interior 20.5 × 14 in, per Ridge.

A tough hard-shell case with a lifetime warranty. Note the listed size is half an inch over the 22 × 14 in US guideline on two sides, so measure it against your airline's sizer if you're worried.

Cotopaxi Allpa 35L Travel Pack

22 × 12 × 10 in; 2 lb 15 oz; water-resistant TPU-coated shell.

A backpack that opens like a suitcase. Better than a roller when you've also got a stroller or a small hand to hold. It's 10 in deep, so don't overstuff it if the airline sizer is 9 in.

Peak Design Packing Cube

Small: 4.5–9 L, 110 g; Medium: 8–18 L, 144 g; both compress to 8 cm deep.

A zip squeezes each cube down to 8 cm deep, so soft clothes take up less room. One cube per family member makes it easy to find things in a shared case.

Bellroy Travel Wallet

Leather, RFID-protected; holds a passport, 4 to 10+ cards, cash, tickets and a Micro Pen.

Keeps everyone's passports and boarding passes in one place, with the pen for landing cards. Bellroy says it fits most passports, though not German or Irish ones.

Kids can sit on it and you tow them through the airport, which saves your back on long walks to the gate. It holds a few days of small-kid clothes plus toys. On Ryanair or easyJet it's bigger than the free under-seat bag.

A small, tough backpack that's easy for a child to carry and small enough for any airline's free under-seat bag.

How to fly carry-on with kids

  • Check each flight, not just the airline. A trip that flies United to London and then Ryanair to Dublin has two very different limits.
  • Pack one cube per person and roll soft clothes.
  • Liquids: security rules vary by airport, so keep liquids in a clear bag you can pull out quickly. Baby milk and food are usually allowed in larger amounts.
  • Wear the bulky stuff (boots and the waterproof jacket) on the plane.
  • Give each child a job: their own backpack with their own snacks and entertainment.

Extra for babies and toddlers

  • A sleep sack (or two for a baby, in case of leaks). Rooms vary a lot, so check the tog guide on the label against the room temperature.
  • Extra changes: plan on two outfits a day for a baby and one spare a day for a toddler, plus extra vests or bodysuits.
  • Bibs: two or three a day, or a few wipe-clean ones you can rinse in the sink.
  • A warm all-in-one or fleece suit for the stroller or carrier on cold, windy days, and a puddle suit once they're walking.

Pack each child's clothes in their own packing cube, with travel-day clothes and the spare outfit on top.

Questions

What size carry-on fits every airline?

There isn't one. US airlines use 22 × 14 × 9 in for the main cabin bag, but on Ryanair the free bag is only 40 × 30 × 20 cm and has to go under the seat. If you're flying a budget European airline, pack each person's essentials in a small under-seat bag and pay for one bigger cabin bag between you.

Can my child bring a Trunki on the plane?

Usually as a cabin bag, yes: Trunki sells it as cabin-sized (46 × 20.5 × 31 cm) and publishes an airline checklist. It's bigger than the free under-seat bag on Ryanair (40 × 30 × 20 cm) and easyJet (45 × 36 × 20 cm), so on those airlines it needs a paid cabin bag allowance. Check your airline.

Are packing cubes worth it for families?

Yes, especially with kids: one cube per person (or per day) means you can find the right pajamas in a dark hotel room without unpacking the whole case. Compression cubes like Peak Design's also squeeze soft clothes down.

Does the Ridge Carry-On meet US carry-on limits?

Ridge lists its Carry-On's exterior at 22.5 × 14.5 × 9 in, which is half an inch over the common 22 × 14 × 9 in limit on two sides (and about 1 cm longer than British Airways' 56 cm). It's sold as a carry-on, but if your airline is strict about the sizer, measure it.

What's the best bag for a kid to carry?

A small backpack they can manage on their own, like the 7 L Fjällräven Kånken Mini, which fits under the seat on budget airlines. Put a water bottle, a snack, a book and a spare layer in it and let them be in charge of it.
