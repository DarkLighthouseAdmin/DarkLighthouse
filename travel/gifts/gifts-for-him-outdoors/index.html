Under $100

SAXX Quest Quick-Dry Mesh Boxer Brief

Quick-dry micro-mesh designed for travel and sink washing. $36 when we checked.

Not exciting to unwrap, but the kind of gift that gets worn every week. Washes in a hotel sink and dries overnight.

Halfday Sidecar Toiletry Kit (Bespoke Post)

8.75 × 3.5 × 6.75 in; 0.68 lb; water-resistant polyester; hanging loop; reusable liquids pouch included. $48 when we checked.

Replaces the plastic grocery bag of toiletries. The hanging loop is the part that wins people over in small bathrooms.

Men & Nails Medium Grooming Tool Kit (Bespoke Post)

12 stainless steel tools in a brown vegan leather case. $60 when we checked.

Nail clippers, scissors and tweezers that live in one case instead of three drawers. A good add-on gift.

Smartwool Men's Classic Thermal Merino Base Layer Crew

100% merino, 250 g/m² midweight. $66 in sale colors (up to $125) when we checked.

A warm layer under a jacket for cold walks and early starts. Sale colors bring the price down a lot, so check a few.

Line of Trade Weekender Bag (Bespoke Post)

Cotton canvas on a metal frame, leather handles, removable shoulder strap; 21 × 13 × 7 in; laptop pocket fits up to 15 in. $75 when we checked.

A canvas duffel that stays open while you pack. It fits the US carry-on guideline, so it works for a weekend away or a short work trip.

Ridge Wallet, Royal Black

Anodized aluminum plates, a scratch-resistant finish and a track that expands for 1 to 12 cards, per Ridge. $76 on sale (regular $95) when we checked.

For the guy with a fat leather wallet he keeps meaning to replace. It forces you down to the cards you actually use.

Darn Tough Men's Hiker Micro Crew 3-Pack

Three pairs of Darn Tough's cushioned merino Hiker Micro Crew in classic colors; $26 a pair bought singly. $78 when we checked.

Merino hiking socks are one of those things people don't buy for themselves. Three pairs means they're not saving them for best.

$100 and up

L.L.Bean Men's Bean Boots, 8"

Made in Maine; L.L.Bean says to order a full size down from your usual shoe size. $150 when we checked.

The classic duck boot for slush, mud and walking the dog. Do read L.L.Bean's sizing note before ordering, because most people get it wrong the first time.

Patagonia Black Hole Duffel 40L

19.7 × 12.5 × 8.5 in; 1,045 g; Patagonia says it fits most airline carry-on requirements. $165 when we checked.

The bag that ends up in the truck, at the gym and in the overhead bin. A safe pick if you don't know his clothing size.

Barbour Transport Wax Jacket (Bespoke Post)

6 oz waxed cotton with a cord collar, tartan lining and an inner storm skirt, in a roomier, more modern cut. $425 when we checked.

The big one. A Barbour gets better with age and can be re-waxed for years. Bespoke Post calls it water-resistant, not waterproof, so it's a jacket for drizzle and wind rather than a downpour.

A few buying tips

  • Sizing: boots and jackets are the gifts most likely to go back. L.L.Bean's Bean Boots run big, and Barbour cuts vary by style, so check the size guide on the product page.
  • Socks and base layers are safe bets. Merino is easy to size and gets worn all winter.
  • Bespoke Post sells single items too. You don't need a subscription to buy from its store.

Questions

Is a Barbour wax jacket waterproof?

Not fully. Bespoke Post describes the Barbour Transport as water-resistant: waxed cotton sheds drizzle and wind well, and it can be re-waxed to keep it that way. For a real downpour, a modern waterproof shell is drier.

How do Bean Boots fit?

Big. L.L.Bean's own product page says to order a full size down from your usual shoe size, and to go by the sizing advice there rather than guessing. If you're buying as a surprise, a gift receipt helps.

What's a good outdoorsy gift under $100?

Merino hiking socks (the Darn Tough 3-pack is $78), a canvas weekender ($75), the Ridge Wallet ($76 on sale when we checked), the SAXX Quest ($36) or a grooming kit ($60).
