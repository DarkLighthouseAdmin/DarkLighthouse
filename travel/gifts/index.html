Questions

How did you pick these gifts?

We started with things that are useful on a trip or in a busy first year with a baby, then checked every price and spec on the retailer's own page. We'd rather list ten good things than fifty.

Are the prices current?

They're what each retailer's own page showed on 23 October 2026. Holiday sales move prices up and down, sometimes daily, so the retailer's page is the final word. If something here looks wrong, it's probably changed since we checked.
