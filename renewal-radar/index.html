What it does

  • Adds up what you actually pay. Weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly charges, turned into one monthly and one yearly number.
  • Warns you before money leaves. A notification before each renewal, and before each free trial turns into a paid plan.
  • Makes cancelling easy. Save the cancel page link with each item. When the reminder pops up, you're one click away.
  • Shows what you've saved. Mark something cancelled and it keeps a running total of what you're no longer paying each year.

Questions

Does it read my email or bank account?

No. You add things yourself. That's a feature: nothing gets access to your inbox or your bank.

Where's my license key after I buy?

In the email Polar sends you right after checkout (check spam if you don't see it), and in your Polar customer portal any time you need it again. Here's how to unlock Pro.

Can I use Pro on more than one computer?

Yes. Paste the same key into Renewal Radar on each browser you use. Your list doesn't sync between them, but you can use Pro's backup and restore to copy it over.

What if I get a new computer?

Back up your list from Settings first, then restore it on the new one and paste your key again.
