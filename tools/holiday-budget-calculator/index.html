What a holiday budget should include

Most holiday overspending isn't the gifts. It's the lines people forget to count: food and hosting, travel, decorations, cards and postage, class and teacher gifts, and a small buffer. Putting each one on its own line is the quickest way to see the real total before December starts.

How the per-paycheck amount works

The formula is (season total − already set aside) ÷ paychecks left before Dec 25. If your list adds up to $1,200, you have $100 set aside and 6 paychecks left, that's $183.34 per paycheck. If that number is more than you can spare, lower the biggest line or the number of gifts, and the amount drops right away.

Telling a real deal from a fake one

A sale tag shows a "was" price, but it's the store's choice what that was. Your own baseline is better: record the price in early November, then compare on the day. As a rule of thumb, under 10% off is rarely worth rushing for, and 20% or more is a clear saving on something you already planned to buy.

Ship-by dates

Carriers post their own holiday cutoffs each year, so check the carrier's site for 2026 dates rather than relying on last year's. The planner has a countdown you can update once you have them.

Questions

How much should I budget for the holidays?

There is no single right number. Start from what you can set aside from now until Dec 25 (paychecks left times an amount you can spare), then split it across gifts, food, travel, decor, cards and hosting. This calculator shows the total of your own list and the amount per paycheck, so you can lower a line until the per-paycheck number fits your budget.

What should a holiday budget include besides gifts?

Food and hosting, travel, decorations, greeting cards and postage, party or school gifts, charity, and a small buffer for things you forgot. Those extras are easy to leave out, so the calculator starts with them as separate lines.

How do I work out how much to save each paycheck?

Take your season total, subtract anything you've already set aside, and divide by the number of paychecks left before Dec 25. Enter your next payday and pay frequency and the calculator counts the paydays for you.

How can I tell if a Black Friday deal is real?

Write down the regular price before the sales begin, then compare it with the sale price on the day. The deal checker shows the real percent off against the price you recorded. Prices change often, so a price you wrote down in early November is a better baseline than the 'was' price on a sale tag.
