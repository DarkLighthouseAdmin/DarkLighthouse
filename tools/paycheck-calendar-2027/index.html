2027 biweekly paycheck calendar

If you're paid every other Friday, you get 26 or 27 paychecks in 2027 depending on your schedule, and some months give you a third one. 2027 starts and ends on a Friday, so the Jan 1 schedule gets 27 paydays and three 3-paycheck months. Find your first 2027 payday below to see which schedule you're on.

Schedule A · 27 paydaysFirst payday Fri, Jan 1. 3-paycheck months: January, July, December.
Schedule B · 26 paydaysFirst payday Fri, Jan 8. 3-paycheck months: April, October.

Months with 3 paychecks in 2027

  • Schedule A (first payday Fri, Jan 1): January, July, December
  • Schedule B (first payday Fri, Jan 8): April, October

Most budgets are built on two paychecks a month, so a third paycheck is free money for savings, debt, or next year's holiday fund. Plan it now so it doesn't disappear.
